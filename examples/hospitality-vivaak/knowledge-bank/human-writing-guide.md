# Human Luxury Hospitality Writing Guide (The Vivaak Standard)

## 1. Tone & Voice
- Evocative, sensory-rich, architectural, serene, and grounded.
- Emphasize private spaces, heated mountain water, mountain air, and curated gastronomy.
- Avoid hyperbole. Let authentic property details create desire.

## 2. Strictly Banned Hospitality Clichés
Sentinel will veto any draft containing:
- "Nestled in the lap of nature"
- "Haven of tranquility"
- "A stone's throw away"
- "Paradise on earth"
- "Look no further"
- "Breathtaking vistas awaiting you"
