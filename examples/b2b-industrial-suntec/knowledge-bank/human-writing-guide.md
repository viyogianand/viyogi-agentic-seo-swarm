# Human B2B Writing Guide (Suntec Manufacturing Standard)

## 1. Tone & Voice
- Factual, engineering-grounded, technical, and precise.
- Speak directly to plant heads, metallurgy engineers, and procurement directors.
- Support every claim with SAE, AMS, or DIN standard numbers.

## 2. Strictly Banned Buzzwords
Sentinel will immediately reject any draft containing:
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Revolutionizing the industry"
- "Game-changer"
- "Cutting-edge excellence"
- "Testament to our dedication"
