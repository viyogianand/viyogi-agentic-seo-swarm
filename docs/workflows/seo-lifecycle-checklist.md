# Conditional SEO Lifecycle Checklist
## Evidence-Driven, Stage-by-Stage SEO Execution & Quality Verification

```
========================================================================================
VIYOGI AGENTIC SEO SWARM — SEO LIFECYCLE CHECKLIST
========================================================================================
```

This checklist provides a structured, conditional lifecycle for executing SEO campaigns. Workflows are selected dynamically based on client business model, domain maturity, and available tool access.

---

## Stage A — Discovery, Access & Knowledge Foundation

- [ ] **A.1 Project Intake:** Run autonomous discovery on repo; pre-populate `project-configuration.json` and `knowledge-bank/`.
- [ ] **A.2 Integration Audit:** Execute `integration-readiness-checklist.md`; identify active vs blocked APIs.
- [ ] **A.3 Knowledge Bank Grounding:** Ingest verified product specs, certifications, and brand facts into Level 1 Memory.
- [ ] **A.4 Negative Constraints Scanner:** Register banned marketing buzzwords and unverified claims in Sentinel (#3).

---

## Stage B — Baseline Search & Competitive Intelligence

- [ ] **B.1 Live Rank Baseline:** Query DataForSEO for initial 50 target keywords across target country SERPs.
- [ ] **B.2 Striking-Distance Identification:** Filter queries ranking between positions **4.0 and 20.0**; score by Opportunity Index.
- [ ] **B.3 Brand SERP Audit:** Verify that official homepage ranks #1 for brand queries; check for entity cannibalization.
- [ ] **B.4 Competitor Heading & Topic Scraping:** Deconstruct top 3 ranking competitor pages; extract entity and table coverage.
- [ ] **B.5 AI Visibility Baseline:** Curate 24-query monitoring panel; measure AI Overview presence and brand citation share.

---

## Stage C — Technical SEO & Architectural Autonomy

- [ ] **C.1 Crawl Budget & Depth Audit:** Confirm that critical product and pillar pages are accessible within $\le 3$ clicks from root.
- [ ] **C.2 Crawl Trap & Route Verification:** Verify that faceted filters and dynamic parameters do not generate infinite URLs.
- [ ] **C.3 Index Bloat Detection:** Scan sitemap routes for thin content ($< 300$ words) or duplicate metadata.
- [ ] **C.4 Canonical & Trailing Slash Alignment:** Audit exact match between `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and `<link rel="canonical">`.
- [ ] **C.5 Structured Data Rich Results:** Validate nested JSON-LD schemas (`Organization`, `Product`, `LocalBusiness`, `FAQPage`) using `validator.py`.
- [ ] **C.6 Instant Broadcasting:** Test automated broadcast pipeline via IndexNow protocol and Google Indexing API.

---

## Stage D — Search Intelligence & Intent Modeling

- [ ] **D.1 Query Fan-Out Graph:** Generate 5-tier directed query tree (Core ➔ Prerequisites ➔ Specs ➔ Applications ➔ Troubleshooting).
- [ ] **D.2 People Also Ask (PAA) Clustering:** Extract and categorize high-intent Google PAA questions into informational vs procurement.
- [ ] **D.3 Information Gain Scoring:** Calculate Information Gain Score ($0–100$) evaluating novelty of data tables, tolerances, and direct answers.
- [ ] **D.4 Keyword Cannibalization Detection:** Scan pairwise page titles and intent overlap; route conflict remediation to `APPROVAL_QUEUE.md`.

---

## Stage E — Editorial Strategy & Content Assembly Line

- [ ] **E.1 Create vs Update Decision:** Editor (#20) decides whether to write a new page, refresh existing content, or consolidate.
- [ ] **E.2 Content Brief Architecture:** Formulate `CONTENT_BRIEF` specifying target entities, required comparison tables, and H2/H3 outline.
- [ ] **E.3 Narrative Copywriting (Scribe):** Draft comprehensive, technically accurate copy adhering to Human Writing Guide.
- [ ] **E.4 On-Page SERP Tuning (Tuner):** Polish Title (<55 chars), Meta Description (<155 chars), and semantic headings.
- [ ] **E.5 AEO Direct-Answer Structuring (Answerer):** Format 40–60 word inverted-pyramid answer blocks and FAQ accordions.
- [ ] **E.6 Schema Formatting (Schema Master):** Build complete `@graph` JSON-LD schemas embedding entities and breadcrumbs.
- [ ] **E.7 Zero-Tolerance Verification (Sentinel):** Verify every factual claim against Archivist; veto hallucinations or schema errors.

---

## Stage F — Authority Acquisition & Linkable Assets

- [ ] **F.1 Linkable Asset Architecture:** Design statistics hubs, calculators, or benchmark data tables (e.g. Ervin fatigue curves) to earn passive editorial links.
- [ ] **F.2 Authority Gap Analysis:** Compare domain backlink intersection against top 3 competitors via DataForSEO Backlinks API.
- [ ] **F.3 Editorial Prospecting:** Identify relevant trade journals, industrial directories, and industry news hubs.
- [ ] **F.4 Value-First Outreach Drafting:** Personalize editorial outreach pitches without mass email spam (Human Approval Gate).

---

## Stage G — Local SEO & Google Business Profile (Where Applicable)

- [ ] **G.1 NAP Consistency Audit:** Align Business Name, Address, and Phone Number across official site, GBP, and maps.
- [ ] **G.2 Local 3-Pack Optimization:** Optimize GBP primary and secondary categories, service areas, and geo-coordinates.
- [ ] **G.3 Geo-Schema Enrichment:** Deploy LocalBusiness / LodgingBusiness JSON-LD markup with Plus Codes and CID links.
- [ ] **G.4 Review Response Drafting:** Draft keyword-rich, authentic responses to customer reviews for operator approval.

---

## Stage H — Measurement, Attribution & Retrospective Calibration

- [ ] **H.1 28-Day Performance Measurement:** Track organic clicks, impressions, rankings, and conversions across experiment window.
- [ ] **H.2 Forecast Calibration:** Compute Prediction Error Score (PES) comparing observed results to P10/P50/P90 targets.
- [ ] **H.3 Memory Promotion:** Promote repeated empirical successes into Level 4 Strategic Rules.
- [ ] **H.4 Executive Reporting:** Compile executive Markdown updates and styled Excel command center dashboards.
